# Peer review: derivation workspace and RSI/GM task selection

Date: 2026-09-26 (America/New_York). Source: consult-6-request-derivation-rsi-gm.md, attachments A–C. Review only: no project code, tests, or experiments executed. No local-subject analysis. This is development feedback, not independent certification of the workspace.

Evidence convention: [measured—reported] identifies observations explicitly reported by the supplied material, without reproduction. [design-only] identifies document analysis, logical examples, proposed specifications, and prospective evaluations; no effectiveness is established. No independently verified literature claims are used. Each label applies to its paragraph or list; table rows carry their own labels. This header is provenance metadata.

[design-only] Recommendation: Use DERIVE as a dependency-aware repair workflow, with a stable acceptance contract and a small independent verification boundary. Use HARDEN for known defects, EXPLORE for genuine uncertainty over routes, and RSI mechanisms only when a recorded failure justifies their overhead. Neither repeated critique nor additional process vocabulary establishes mathematical validity. The current addendum contains several contract conflicts that should be resolved before its promotion states are trusted.

## A1. The repair unit is a dependency slice, not an isolated anchor

[design-only] Claim-grain repair is appropriate for a wrong citation, local algebraic slip, or missing condition whose dependency closure is small and explicit. The correct unit is the anchor plus its premises, definitions, and affected downstream uses. A-1's restriction to the anchor and cells citing it can omit unrecorded semantic dependencies. A-2's refuter seeing only the revised anchor and original finding cannot assess those dependencies. Preserve blindness to the writer's self-assessment and prior verdicts, not blindness to the mathematical premises.

[design-only] Require whole-trace re-verification when a revision changes the statement, quantifiers, conditioning sigma-algebra, exchangeability class, null transformation, coordinate/measure convention, or a shared definition; when dependencies are cyclic or unknown; or when a local repair changes the interpretation of another step. For example, replacing unconditional exchangeability with within-stratum exchangeability may change the transformation group, reference distribution, and final rejection rule. That is one coupled repair, not several unrelated findings.

[design-only] Resolve the editing conflict explicitly: the initiator freezes trace-table cells, while A-1 permits revising cells and A-5 accepts a changed trace. Under the supplied pin, keep old trace cells immutable and append a versioned replacement trace or authorized correction overlay. Build the effective trace deterministically from those records. If the pin does not permit this representation, request a pin-compatible amendment; do not silently reinterpret an immutable cell as editable.

[design-only] Two consecutive "nothing new" responses are a stopping heuristic, not a fixed-point certificate. Stop proposing local repairs when the bounded budget is exhausted or no new adjudicated issue appears. Then run the reserved independent Check over the effective trace and affected companions. Pass requires the actual acceptance obligations; a third round ending without a valid final Check remains unresolved. Specify round precedence so "nothing new twice" neither automatically promotes the file nor overrides a still-open defeater.

## A2. Prediction-registered VERIFY measures the verifier first

[design-only] The prediction sheet primarily measures the verifier's ability to anticipate independently adjudicated findings, and secondarily diagnoses unclear derivation interfaces. It does not measure proof truth directly. "Which anchor moves under a better substrate" is not a testable prediction until "better substrate," the permitted intervention, deadline, and outcome definition are fixed. "Survives unchanged" is also weaker than correctness: a wrong claim can survive an ineffective review.

[design-only] Gameable sheets select easy rows, omit uncertain ones, predict broad defect categories that fit anything, or let the predicting verifier adjudicate its own success. CONFIRMED/MISS without a false-alarm category rewards predicting defects everywhere. Declaring a difficult prediction unfalsifiable must not erase it from the instrument's performance denominator. Writer self-assessment isolation helps, but historical lesson files and the same judge key can still reveal likely outcomes.

[design-only] No scoring rule is immune to an incomplete sampling frame or biased reference judgments. The smallest defensible protocol is: freeze a finite set of load-bearing row/type questions independently of the predictor; require a probability for each; lock predictions before downstream inspection; and score against separately adjudicated binary outcomes using mean Brier loss, mean((p − y)^2). For example, "Does row R illegally use a numerical match to discharge universal validity?" has a more definite outcome than "Will R improve?"

[design-only] Record adjudication uncertainty separately and report the unadjudicated fraction. Do not treat unresolved ground truth as a negative finding. If predictions can abstain, preregister minimum coverage and report loss together with coverage; otherwise abstention can hide the hardest cases. Start with synthetic traces whose defects and valid controls are independently specified. Proper scoring discourages probability misreporting under a fixed valid outcome process; it does not repair a bad outcome process.

[design-only] For this workspace, make prediction sheets optional instrument-calibration work, sampled on ambiguous or newly introduced rules. Requiring them on every ordinary derivation adds a second evaluation task whose cost needs justification. Only the independently supported findings, not predictive accuracy, should affect the derivation's grade.

## A3. Defeater panels can confidently block or pass the wrong trace

[design-only] A confident wrong block occurs when both attackers interpret a conditional theorem as unconditional, omit a premise, or demand unavailable empirical support for an explicitly assumed mathematical hypothesis. A key written before the trace can encode the same category error. The judge then marks VALID, and A-4 blocks a correct statement. Require every proposed defeater to identify the exact claim, relevant premise set, alleged violated obligation, and a witness or explicit reasoning chain. Keep proposed, substantiated, adjudicated-valid, rejected, and unresolved statuses separate.

[design-only] A confident wrong pass occurs when attackers and judge share a blind spot—for example, treating invariance of a statistic as sufficient for exchangeability of its null distribution—or when a polished move trace hides an invalid conditioning step. Two blind generators may still have correlated errors. "No VALID defeater found" is a bounded search result, not a completeness theorem. Independent proof obligations and targeted controls remain necessary even after unanimous panel approval.

[design-only] Of the six types, boundary-UNDECIDABLE is least decidable as currently named: the packet gives no operational criterion for distinguishing mathematical undecidability, missing evidence, and an under-specified scope boundary. Reserve formal undecidability language for a stated theorem; otherwise use scope-unresolved or evidence-unavailable. MISATTRIBUTION and support availability can often be grounded in records; values-for-logic can often be localized to an illegal inference; vacuity and debt-freeness still require semantic inspection.

[design-only] Resolve the registry/initiator contradiction: §D calls the defeater criteria UNCALIBRATED and advisory, while K-defeaters is required and A-4 makes panel VALID blocking. Until calibration, panel outputs are proposals for adjudication under the existing trusted contract. A credible challenge may suspend applicability pending that adjudication; the uncalibrated panel must not manufacture authoritative VALID labels. Calibration needs both known-valid traces and independently seeded defects, not a single purported gold trace alone.

[design-only] Forced ranking may order repair effort, but should not determine validity or pressure a panel to invent a "best defect." Allow an empty accepted-defeater set. Define an appeal path using the full relevant dependency slice, with resolution evidence and a bounded cost; otherwise false blocks can persist indefinitely.

## A4. Replay forks test recoverability by a model, not proof validity

[design-only] A fork asks whether a specified model, context package, prompt, and budget can reconstruct a move. Success is evidence about recoverability under that protocol; failure may reflect missing context or model capability. Neither establishes whether the mathematical move is valid, nor whether a human historically originated it. A withheld next-step test cannot reconstruct causal authorship from counterfactual model behavior.

[design-only] "Human/model/joint/unrecorded" describes provenance, while "crystallized" describes the relationship between an idea and a later commitment. These are not naturally exclusive classes. Use separate axes: recorded proposer/executor; precursor present/absent/uncertain; and fork outcome correct alternative/correct same route/invalid/no completed route. Score logical equivalence rather than exact wording. A factual package check should confirm the necessary definitions and premises, not just filenames.

[design-only] For statistics and spatial nulls, forks are worth considering when a decisive move repeatedly fails to transfer between agents: recognizing the appropriate transformation group, conditioning away a nuisance while preserving a testable target, or detecting a singleton null orbit. A successful fork may suggest clearer documentation or a reusable lemma. Routine algebra, already explicit finite enumeration, and simple formatting checks do not justify a model-fork experiment.

[design-only] Start with one preselected disputed step and two inexpensive fresh-context forks, followed by one independent adjudication if no mechanical check exists: at most three model calls. Classify this as a documentation/transfer diagnostic. Do not launch recursive retries until the preferred historical move reappears; stop if the next result would not change the derivation instructions or route choice.

## A5. Canary pairs must target four different failure classes

[design-only] One dropped-denominator pair is insufficient for the recorded policy defects. Use a tiny suite with each valid/control pair identical except for the targeted fault. Mathematical controls test inference; workflow controls test legal promotion. The same acceptance path must detect both kinds without confusing them.

| Failure class | Valid control | Defective twin and required response | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vacuous hypothesis | A proposed null-validity certificate states exchangeability under a fixed transformation group, with an explicit way that assumption can fail. | Replace the condition with "assume the test is valid" and claim the same certification. Reject certification as circular; a transparently conditional tautology may be logically true but cannot discharge the original obligation. | design-only |
| Values standing in for logic | A finite synthetic null is fully enumerated; a separate argument establishes why its transformations preserve the stipulated distribution. | Retain matching numerical outputs but delete the preservation argument and claim general validity. Mark that logical obligation unresolved even if all sampled values match. | design-only |
| EXEC row doing typed work | An execution row records a value; a distinct permitted certifying row supplies the logical discharge and supporting basis. | Move the discharge, cross-step logical reference, or certification token into EXEC. Require the appropriate structural/semantic failure, regardless of the final value. | design-only |
| State promotion without event | A promotion request points to an independent successful VERIFY event for the exact candidate version, plus any required oracle rerun. | Change only State: or reference a stale/different-version VERIFY. Refuse the promotion; preserve the artifact's prior certified state. | design-only |

[design-only] Controls must not be accepted by memorizing their IDs or rejected merely because they contain the word "canary." Keep frozen test payloads, independently check the expected verdict, and vary superficial representation in occasional held-out controls. A valid-pair rejection exposes an overblocking evaluator; an invalid-pair acceptance exposes an underblocking one. Treat either as an instrument problem, not automatically as a defect in the target derivation.

[design-only] The state test reveals a potential bootstrap ambiguity: the candidate must have an independent VERIFY before promotion, but the verifier also grades State discipline. Verify the candidate at its current state and issue a promotion authorization as a separate event; then update the displayed State through the controller. A promise that verification will occur later cannot satisfy the event requirement.

## A6. Numeric oracle semantics: distinguish contradiction from inapplicability

[design-only] Preserve every oracle output, but do not call every mismatch the same thing. A comparable value outside a preregistered tolerance is a failed numerical obligation requiring investigation. A unit, domain, input-version, or assumption mismatch means the result is inapplicable to the claimed obligation; suspend its use. An execution error or missing input yields unchecked/error. A match supports only the declared value at the declared scope. A validated counterexample can refute a universal claim; isolated numeric agreement cannot establish one.

[design-only] The minimum oracle contract records: contract and implementation version; target claim and row IDs; input artifact references and schema; quantity, unit, coordinate/measure convention, domain, and conditioning assumptions; preprocessing; deterministic or stochastic mode and seed plan; numerical tolerance and comparison rule; expected coverage; permitted conclusion; prohibited extrapolations; and trusted execution receipt. Numeric conditioning and precision belong in the tolerance rationale, not in an after-the-fact exception.

[design-only] Mechanical decidability comes from typed equality or declared conversions between the claim manifest and the oracle manifest: unit, band, side, input version, and support domain either match or do not. Semantic applicability still requires a separately supported mapping from the mathematical claim to that manifest. Missing assumptions are UNKNOWN, not inferred agreement. "Contract mismatch is decidable" must mean the declared-field check is decidable, not that a checker understands all unstated mathematics.

[design-only] Respect the document's export restrictions: public derivations can use permitted opaque evidence references, while any protected content identities stay in an authorized controller ledger. Do not solve provenance by copying prohibited hashes or protected contents into the derivation. Within this review, no such inputs are needed.

[design-only] K-oracle may remain optional only when no required claim depends on its result and the requested state does not require oracle checking. Conditionalize the gate: if an oracle supports a required value claim or an oracle-checked promotion, its applicable successful receipt becomes required. A failed optional oracle must still trigger examination if it contradicts a required claim; "optional" is not permission to ignore contrary evidence.

## A7. Territory and incubation: measure distinct tests, not filled boxes

[design-only] A mandatory outside-pentad proposal encourages exploration only if "family" denotes a substantively different assumption, representation, transformation, or proof strategy. Requiring F6 on every ticket can instead produce renamed duplicates and irrelevant abstractions. Require a bounded outside-family search attempt when route uncertainty matters, then permit none found within budget as a coverage result. It must not block an otherwise valid single-route proof unless outside-family exploration is an explicit task objective.

[design-only] The three-return escalation rule is vulnerable to automatic rereading: every run rereads the ledger, so an item can accumulate "returns" without new evidence. Increment return counts only for substantive reactivation caused by changed prerequisites or a new discriminating argument. Store the trigger and its evidence. Priority should reflect expected unblock value, relevance, and cost; repeated rediscovery of an unresolved idea is a signal to diagnose the blocker, not automatically to promote it.

[design-only] After three runs, an auditor needs: ticket and route IDs; registry version; assumptions and representation; ancestry; the concrete difference from existing families; attempted discriminatory check; result including unknown; resource cost; whether it changed an accepted claim or exposed a new limitation; and a blocked-route resume condition. Exploration is demonstrated descriptively by genuinely distinct tested routes and consequential findings. Repeated F6 relabeling, unchanged walls, and escalating return counts without a new test indicate ritual. Three runs alone do not establish causal benefit.

[design-only] Correct C-3's universal corroboration ordering. A Lean proof and numeric oracle do not independently establish the same proposition when the oracle verifies only values. Two blind writers can share a false premise or proof pattern. Record which obligation each route supports, shared dependencies, and the distinct failure modes tested; do not automatically rank two shallow routes above one complete proof. Also distinguish corroborated-by as a relationship from the pin's tightly constrained attestation: corroborated field. Neither should silently confer the other.

## B9. Which RSI mechanisms earn their overhead?

| Mechanism | Decision and trigger | Benefit sought; cost or failure to avoid | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Archive + parent sampling | Keep a minimal provenance archive; enable sampling only after genuinely distinct viable routes exist. | Recover a discarded useful branch. Do not load the full archive into every prompt or resample cosmetic variants. | design-only |
| Quality-diversity niches | Gate behind premature convergence or a task explicitly requiring alternatives. | Preserve meaningfully different assumptions or methods. For a single fixed theorem, mandatory family quotas add bookkeeping without proof value. | design-only |
| Co-evolving red-team bank | Keep calibrated core controls; add probes after a new failure class is observed. | Cover discovered blind spots. Freeze evaluation cohorts and keep independent final controls; candidate-adapted probes alone cannot validate improvement. | design-only |
| Reflexion lessons | Keep short evidence-linked lessons for recurring failure patterns; expire them after premise/instrument changes. | Prevent repeated mistakes. Do not turn one failed regime into a universal ban or leak held-out feedback through memory. | design-only |
| STOP playbook rewrite | Gate behind a documented plateau and an explicit policy hypothesis. | Change proposal strategy when the old strategy fails. Rewrite proposals at boundaries, test them against a frozen baseline, and separate operator changes from utility changes. | design-only |
| Final debate/refutation | Keep one focused independent refutation for load-bearing unresolved semantic steps; extra rounds require distinct findings. | Catch a missed assumption or illegal inference. Majority agreement and eloquence do not replace proof; endless debate adds cost without a new test. | design-only |

[design-only] The practical dividing line is whether the bottleneck is repairing a known obligation or finding a useful route/diagnostic that is not yet represented. HARDEN is economical for the first; selected exploration and instrument-development mechanisms can help with the second. If the bottleneck is unavailable support or an invalid acceptance contract, neither loop should continue scoring candidates as if the evidence existed.

## B10. Self-application needs an evaluator outside the modified mechanism

[design-only] A loop improving its own prose spec establishes, at most, that a selected artifact changed and passed the chosen checks. It becomes evidence of operational improvement only when a frozen baseline and candidate are compared on tasks and failure injections whose assessment is not controlled by the candidate. Fixing a known bug is evidence for that bug class, not general recursive self-improvement. Keep separate claims for specification correctness, controller reliability, task quality, and resource efficiency.

[design-only] Minimal pilot: freeze the baseline; let development tasks produce one candidate; then evaluate both on two previously unseen synthetic task classes—one derivation containing a missing assumption and one controller workflow receiving late invalidating evidence. Include a valid counterpart in each class to detect always-block behavior. Run two repetitions per baseline/candidate/task: eight runs, with a preregistered common cost ceiling and final checks owned by an external evaluator. Keep paired task inputs and randomization records. This is a descriptive pilot; broad superiority requires broader independent evaluation.

[design-only] Predeclare the primary outcome and acceptable regressions: supported completion rate, false completion/false block, exact state-transition integrity, total billed resources, and human interventions. Report all runs, not only successful keeps. If the candidate only improves spend while retaining outcome quality, claim cost improvement on that suite. If it only fixes the injected bug, claim that scoped fix. Repeated selection against the same "held-out" suite consumes it as development evidence; a subsequent claim needs a fresh assessment or an explicit reuse analysis.

[design-only] The candidate must not self-modify the external acceptance standard, sealed evaluation cases or keys, approval/authentication root, audit history, resource meter, authorized budget ceiling, or its own access permissions. Trusted-controller changes may be proposed as candidate artifacts, but evaluation runs under a separate immutable supervisor. Authorized human amendments create a new experimental contract; they must not retroactively certify earlier failures. The prover and checker may be improved as research objects, but neither can grant itself trust while being evaluated.

## B11. What I need explicit, and what should remain principles

[design-only] For a derivation, I need an executable-in-meaning task contract: the exact proposition and quantifiers; scope and permitted assumptions; authoritative pin/version; authorized evidence channels; the requested promotion state; mandatory versus conditional obligations; edit permissions; dependency representation; oracle authority; who can adjudicate and promote; and a bounded resource plan. A TOC-grade brief is insufficient if it omits a premise or the distinction between a numerical check and a proof. I can propose the reasoning method; I cannot safely invent the acceptance semantics.

[design-only] I would require these concrete template corrections before faithful execution:

- Stable rubric: Keep K1–K20's normative meaning fixed for an evaluation cohort. "Fresh key per round" should mean fresh private application/check items under that rubric, not a newly invented standard. Rule changes require a new cohort and applicable rechecks.
- Mechanical versus semantic obligations: K2 shape, K3 fenced fields, anchor resolution, enum membership, and parts of K9/K20 should use commands where available. Judges still inspect the mathematical meaning. Do not spend three judges on properties a parser already settles.
- Grade-to-DONE mapping: Define whether ACCEPT-WITH-DEBT meets this run's objective. If all K-items are required, it cannot quietly substitute for GOLD-READY. Non-load-bearing debt is permissible only when the requested state explicitly allows it.
- Promotion transaction: VERIFY assesses an identified candidate before promotion; the controller records an authorized state transition afterward. File existence and a State string do not substitute for the event.
- Repair scope and immutability: Replace anchor-only isolation with a dependency slice; resolve frozen trace cells using the pin's authorized append/correction method. Supply premises to fresh refuters while withholding self-assessment and verdict history.
- Calibrated defeater authority: Until panel calibration, its findings require trusted adjudication. Define proposed versus VALID and provide evidence-based resolution of disputed blocks.
- Conditional oracle requirement: An oracle may be optional for a purely logical footprint, but required for claims or state transitions that rely on it. Preserve failed and inapplicable outcomes distinctly.
- Real budget units: Six "rounds" plus one reserved round does not specify admission's writer/three judges, two attackers/one adjudicator, refuters, and final VERIFY. State whether the reserve is within six, and reserve actual calls/resources for final verification before allocating repairs.

[design-only] I would keep as principles: seek counterexamples before polishing; select a useful mathematical representation; identify the strongest live objection; prefer a small claim that can be established; choose a second route when it challenges a distinct assumption; and record a surprise only when it changes the reasoning or next test. Personas, forced outside-family ideas, prediction sheets on every run, and repeated blind panels should be optional methods selected for a reason.

[design-only] The short instruction "use RQGM and RSI loops" can guide an internal drafting-and-critique process. It does not tell me which source is authoritative, whether a trace cell may be changed, what evidence permits promotion, or how to treat an oracle mismatch. Spell out those boundaries, then allow flexibility in how the derivation is found. That is the smallest useful combination of capable-model judgment and independently checkable discipline for this workspace.
