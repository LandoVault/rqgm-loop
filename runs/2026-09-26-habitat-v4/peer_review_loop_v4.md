# Peer review: loop architecture v4

Date: 2026-09-26 (America/New_York). Target: the verbatim v4 design in consult-4-request-loop-v4.md. Scope: architecture review only; no repository implementation, tests, experiments, or patient-data analysis performed. This review is development feedback, not a held-out assessment.

Evidence convention: [measured—reported] denotes measurements described in the supplied brief, not independently inspected logs or reproduced results. [literature—unverified] denotes claims attributed to papers by that brief; attempted retrieval of four central papers failed, so their text and numerical claims were not independently verified. [design-only] denotes document analysis, counterexamples, mathematical constructions, and proposed changes; none establishes v4 effectiveness. Labels apply to the entire paragraph, list item, or table row they introduce. Scope and provenance statements above are administrative metadata.

[design-only] Verdict: Retain the three modes, but revise the acceptance and execution contract before treating v4 as enforceable. The largest risk is accepting a result from an invalid instrument merely because its evaluator kind ranks highly. The next risks are mistaking advisory checks for enforced transitions, and admitting or exiting runs on insufficiently checked baseline results. A smaller controller with explicit dependencies is preferable to adding another autonomous loop.

## 1. Three-loop decomposition

[design-only] HARDEN / EXPLORE / DERIVE is a useful division of work objects, not yet evidence for three independent orchestration systems. Implement one controller with three modes: HARDEN repairs an artifact, EXPLORE allocates a candidate portfolio, and DERIVE repairs a claim dependency. DERIVE should normally be a bounded HARDEN subroutine, with standalone entry only for an independently scoped derivation. Add an instrument-calibration state, not a fourth recursive search loop: an invalid instrument suspends dependent decisions until a separately authorized version is calibrated. The router becomes expensive if every finding spawns an agent; mechanically route schema errors, batch citation and propagation fixes at boundaries, deduplicate repeated findings, and charge nested repairs to the same run budget. Rank-based severity must not determine truth: every unresolved valid defeater of a required claim blocks acceptance, even when ranked fourth or lower.

Define interrupt as immediately suspending affected evaluation/acceptance and queued dependent work; it need not mean immediately spawning a repair agent. Boundary work cannot leave a known-invalid result eligible for keep or exit.

## 3. Evaluator ladder and minimum canaries

[design-only] Replace the strict ladder with claim-scoped authority plus instrument validity. Lean establishes a proposition under its formal assumptions; it does not establish that the proposition encodes the intended scientific claim. A command establishes its programmed checks; a primary source can reveal that those checks encode the wrong units or target. A lower-ranked finding must be able to suspend applicability of a higher-ranked result without rewriting that result from pass to fail. Preserve "the theorem compiled," but mark "supports criterion C" invalid or unresolved. An axiom audit is useful evidence, not a proof of semantic correspondence.

[design-only] A command becomes gameable whenever candidate selection optimizes a proxy with blind spots: omitted cases, weak aggregates, hard-coded visible anchors, untested branches, or a scorer authored using earlier candidate feedback. Freezing the script prevents subsequent edits; it does not make its original design independent or valid. kind=command should require a criterion-to-test contract, domain and assumptions, immutable scorer and dependency hashes, case-manifest hash, expected coverage, aggregation rule, and calibration evidence. A scorer-generated result must bind those identifiers to candidate bytes, environment, inputs, outputs, errors, exclusions, and time. A scorer change starts a new evaluation cohort; old and new scores are not directly comparable without re-evaluation.

[design-only] The smallest useful canary is a matched valid/invalid pair, evaluated through the same acceptance path. One known-bad case alone cannot distinguish a working evaluator from an always-rejecting one. Canary outcomes must be recorded separately from the target's quality, and controls must not be supplied as optimization targets. These pairs diagnose specific defects, not general reliability:

- [design-only] Derivation: A valid elementary identity with an explicit domain, paired with a version that drops an essential nonzero-denominator assumption. Require rejection or unresolved status for the latter and acceptance of the valid version. Separately verify that the checked statement is the submitted statement; rejection of a decoy theorem does not protect a different target. If an authorized repair removes the false claim, that repaired artifact may pass; the immutable defective control must still fail.
- [design-only] Scorer-gated search: One independently specified synthetic fixture satisfying the contract and one paired violation that superficially improves the optimized score—for example, omitting a required difficult case. The coverage/acceptance gate must reject the omission and accept the complete valid fixture. Compare exact case inventories, not aggregate score alone.
- [design-only] Prose specification: Two short specs identical except that one contains an explicit contradictory required transition, such as allowing COMPLETE while a required criterion is unchecked. Acceptance must distinguish them using the frozen rubric. Blind order and one AB/BA presentation pair diagnose order sensitivity; agreement does not establish general semantic accuracy.

[literature—unverified] The brief attributes evaluator hierarchies and PROCTOR-style canaries to arXiv:2607.07663 and arXiv:2609.02246, and specific family/order effects to arXiv:2609.17857. Those attributions are not independently established here. The recommendations above do not depend on their reported numerical effects.

## 4. Admission: protect verified behavior, not a lucky pass

[design-only] Counterexample: S passes a scorer on one visible seed while failing an untested path; its criterion becomes protected and the run exits COMPLETE(S). Another failure occurs when a setup-rung pass is treated as a final-rung pass. Conversely, a correct redesign might require temporarily breaking a nonessential proxy: freezing every passed intermediate property can block the only valid repair. Protection should cover required final behavior and declared invariants at acceptance, not every intermediate feature of S.

[design-only] Minimal replacement rule: "A criterion is protected only after S receives a valid Check at that criterion's required acceptance scope. Fail and unchecked/uncertain are eligible for bounded repair or evaluation; no unchecked required criterion permits completion. Protection is rechecked after changes to its dependency closure. COMPLETE(S) satisfies the same final contract as loop completion." For stochastic criteria, preregister a small independent replication/case plan and the pass rule; do not repeatedly sample until a pass appears. No fixed small replication count guarantees reliability.

[design-only] Should held-out judges check S? Yes for required semantic judge-based criteria, using the same final rubric and exposure policy as loop outputs; no as a universal extra layer over valid mechanical checks. Use development evaluators for provisional admission and a separate final held-out assessment for completion. Once an evaluator's detailed feedback is used for repair, it is development feedback, regardless of its original name. Rejecting S at final evaluation either ends that assessment attempt or consumes a preregistered holdout-refresh policy; it must not silently turn a sealed test into a training loop.

[design-only] Resolve three internal ambiguities: §3 says S has "no judges" although some criteria require judges; §4.6 references HARDEN admission without distinguishing candidate eligibility from the S-failure gate; and EXPLORE tests only its frontier during admission although §1 also requires a candidate eligible for HARDEN. Define baseline generation without deliberative judges, followed by the criterion-specific Check. Define eligible_for_harden(candidate) separately from needs_harden(candidate). A completed candidate can bypass HARDEN; a diverse portfolio with no eligible candidate cannot claim the stated EXPLORE success.

[measured—reported] The brief reports that S matched or beat loop arms at about 1/40 of subagent tokens on two small tasks, with one observation per cell and one model family. This motivates a baseline; it does not measure the effectiveness of the proposed admission policy or establish that loops generally lose.

## 5. EXPLORE: lanes, lineage, and roots × depth

### 5a. The lanes are a heuristic, not specified parallel tempering

[design-only] Rename §4.2 "exploration/exploitation lanes" unless a temperature model is genuinely needed. Novelty admission, promotion after a cold Check, and a best-novelty restart do not specify replica sampling or a temperature-dependent swap kernel. The hot lane also inherits every command property its parent passes, which can prevent it from crossing a proxy-quality valley. Split hard feasibility/safety invariants from optimizable quality gates: both lanes obey the former; the hot lane may temporarily fail the latter but remains ineligible for accepted output.

[design-only] If a mathematical temperature analogue is required, define a common energy E(x), inverse temperatures βc > βh ≥ 0, and target densities πβ(x) proportional to exp(−βE(x)) on the same feasible state space. Exchanging hot state xh and cold state xc has acceptance probability:

αswap = min(1, exp((βc − βh) × (E(xc) − E(xh)))).

[design-only] This expression follows from the ratio of the joint target densities after and before exchange. Within-lane proposals also need valid transition kernels, including proposal-probability corrections when asymmetric. Free-form LLM proposals generally do not supply those probabilities, so adding this swap equation alone does not justify a sampling claim. Different novelty and quality energies require the full cross-density ratio. For this repo, the heuristic name is the smaller and more auditable choice.

### 5b. Headers cannot establish ancestry or diversity

[design-only] The slot rules limit some obvious concentration, but do not prevent collapse. Two distinct parents can share one root; every nonfavoured slot can converge on the same alternative root; renaming headlines defeats a headline-identity check; operator names can be aliases. A header can assert a root or family, but cannot establish it. Resolve parent content hashes in the controller's ancestry graph, derive roots transitively, and include all parents for recombination. Keep root diversity distinct from semantic family diversity, which needs a frozen registry and adjudicated assignments.

[design-only] Define K and the denominator: proposed slots, valid evaluated candidates, survivors, and final portfolio are different populations. For K=4, a cap of ceil(K/3)=2 permits 50%, inconsistent with a final 0.34 maximum if applied to that same population. Use count ≤ floor(0.34 × N) for that portfolio constraint, and define a separate feasible slot allocation. Preregister what happens when constraints are infeasible; return blocked, not silent relaxation. Reserve at least two independent roots where the budget permits, plus an explicit fresh-root slot. Specify whether "no operator on its own child" prohibits consecutive use of an operator or identity of the generating agent.

### 5c. Choose the grid from cost and coverage before candidates

[design-only] Roots × depth is a useful budget allocation, but §6 encodes one pair rather than an actual grid of alternatives, and epochs are unequal units when evaluation or DERIVE costs differ. Before seeing candidates, set a total cost ceiling, reserve final assessment and closure, declare candidate-generation/evaluation allowances, and choose feasible allocations from the declared family coverage requirement. If four families are mandatory, the baseline must be asked for four, not merely "≥3." Root counts do not establish independent ideas; record generator context and ancestry as well as root IDs.

[design-only] For a comparative experiment, preregister a small set such as 1×12 versus 4×3 only after pricing the same generation, evaluation, and control work in both arms. Fix initial problem statements, stopping rules, allocation policy, and handling of blocked roots. Report quality, valid front additions, coverage, false completion, and total cost—not front moves alone, which can reward churn. Use synthetic-only independent repetitions if later making comparative claims; one realization remains descriptive. Any adaptive allocation is a preregistered policy, not an undocumented grid change.

[literature—unverified] The brief cites arXiv:2606.10587 for tempering-inspired hypothesis search and arXiv:2609.19799 for roots/depth effects. Neither paper was retrievable in this review; no paper-specific equivalence or optimal grid is asserted.

## 6. Probe lifecycle: correctness, usefulness, and redundancy

[design-only] "Silent on identity" tests one preservation property, not specificity across valid cases. "Fires on its own seeded defect" can reward an overfitted signature chosen by the probe author. Calibration needs an independently constructed defect of the same class and a valid negative control. Define units, signs, operating range, and expected responses; <0.5 × response is ambiguous for zero/negative values and dimensionally meaningless across incompatible metrics. A probe that correctly exposes a defect in the planted twin should challenge the twin's validity rather than automatically invalidate itself.

[design-only] Replace the universal 0.5 threshold with probe-specific acceptance intervals fixed before activation: valid-control false alarms, detection of intended defects, and explicitly bounded influence on unrelated measurements. If controls are stochastic, specify repetitions and uncertainty; if deterministic, record exact expected outcomes or tolerances. Calibration failures remain nonactive, while harness execution errors receive separate error status. Calibration on visible controls does not establish validity on every future candidate.

[design-only] Two epochs with zero firings means "not observed to fire on this candidate distribution," not saturated or useless. It may be a valuable sentinel after its defect has been eliminated. Keep a small mandatory core of probes guarding required failure classes, rotate other probes through dormant and active states, and retain reactivation conditions. Never-fired probes should be sampled according to explicit coverage needs; retirement cannot quietly delete coverage of a required failure mode.

[design-only] A correct but redundant probe detects the same defect cases as an existing probe, with no additional severity or coverage information on the calibration matrix. Store each probe's case-by-case detection vector and compare incremental coverage, disagreement cases, and execution cost. Identical columns establish redundancy only on that matrix, not semantic equivalence everywhere. Keep the cheaper representative active, retain the other dormant, and require independent challenge cases before retiring the last guard for a defect class. Co-firing on candidate history alone is insufficient because the history is selected by the current search.

[design-only] Instrument updates require an immediate quarantine rule and a boundary activation rule. Rebaseline all incumbents eligible for comparison, not merely the named anchors; otherwise a new child faces a different test than its retained parent. Preserve separate evaluation cohorts and prevent keep/exit while an affected incumbent has only stale results. Fixed visible anchors are regression controls, not held-out validation.

## 7. Decisions the archive still cannot reconstruct

[design-only] The excerpt names inherited record types but does not supply their full schemas. The following are required fields/invariants not established by the supplied v4 text; inherited schemas may already contain some. Each affects a keep, halt, or exit:

- [design-only] Exact accepted object: Candidate, target tree, derivation, source snapshot, criterion, scorer/dependencies, bank, and case-manifest hashes bound into one evaluation cohort. Filenames and version labels alone cannot show what passed.
- [design-only] Why a keep won: Eligible alternatives, old incumbent, absolute per-criterion outcomes, uncertainty/error treatment, pairwise order, tie-break rule, protected regressions, and new incumbent. The second keep must name the artifact after the first keep and the joint recheck result.
- [design-only] Which defects blocked acceptance: Stable finding and claim IDs, scope, dependency links, severity, validity, all valid defeaters rather than rank1 alone, disposition evidence, and waiver authority. A top-three count cannot reconstruct absence of a fourth blocking defeater.
- [design-only] Why a route happened in time: Monotonic event IDs, observed-at and processed-at epoch/phase, parent route, suspension state, spawned-work budget, and resolution. Current routes cannot prove that an interrupt did not wait.
- [design-only] Why diversity gates passed: Controller-derived ancestry, complete parent sets, registry version, family assignments, slot reservations, population denominator, and cumulative quota counters. Self-declared root and in_favoured are insufficient.
- [design-only] Why an instrument was trusted: Calibration case results, activation authorization, contamination/exposure status, affected-result invalidations, and completed rebaseline comparisons. anchor_rebaseline needs referenced outcomes, not a boolean.
- [design-only] Whether the next action was affordable: budget.reserved is promised in §9 but absent from §6's setup schema. Record total and component ceilings, committed reservations, actual charges, retries, and final-assessment reserve. Null token totals cannot authorize spending against a token ceiling; choose an observable enforceable resource instead.
- [design-only] Whether replay duplicated work: Attempt/call IDs, dispatch/result/commit state, cached-result references, and reconciliation after a crash. "Re-run the unfinished epoch" can repeat finished expensive calls. A torn-tail recovery needs evidence of what was discarded; JSON shape alone cannot establish single-writer integrity or contemporaneous logging.
- [design-only] Why the run could exit or resume: Immutable contract revision, authorized final rung, full frontier-plus-eligibility predicate, final Checks, held-out exposure history, all OPEN dispositions, approvals, and closure cost. Resolve "no record after exit" versus resume/correction: use a linked new run or an explicitly permitted post-exit audit stream.

[design-only] Also resolve §6's ambiguous "archive is private to EXPLORE generators": state explicitly that it is withheld from those generators. Logging access grants and input manifests supports audits, although it cannot prove model-context freshness or unrecorded human behavior. Historical replay must distinguish absent evidence from a proved violation; a structurally valid reconstructed archive is not evidence of an original enforced run.

## 9. Alternative architectures

### A. Controller-owned finite-state workflow

[design-only] One controller owns immutable artifacts, job dispatch, reservations, state transitions, and commit authorization. Agents return proposals, findings, or evaluation payloads; they do not advance the run. HARDEN / EXPLORE / DERIVE are bounded job types on one dependency graph. Instrument calibration is a quarantined state. This would beat v4 when interruption, nested work, or parallelism makes auditing after the fact more costly than enforcing transitions upfront. Its advantage must be demonstrated through avoided invalid transitions and recoverable interruptions, not assumed from additional infrastructure. It needs no general distributed platform.

### B. Mechanical-gates research workbench

[design-only] Accept only explicitly formalized, synthetic, or executable criteria. Human review scopes contracts and semantic claims; LLMs may propose artifacts and counterexamples but never supply final acceptance marks. Unsupported semantic claims remain unresolved. This would beat v4 where the artifact class has adequate independent mechanical tests and LLM judging dominates cost or inconsistency. It would lose on prose or scientific correspondence questions that the formalization cannot represent. Removing judges does not remove scorer-validity obligations or turn test coverage into universal correctness.

### C. Single agent plus independent controls

[design-only] Start with S, matched canaries, and a final criterion-specific Check. Allow one bounded repair pass only for explicit failures; invoke EXPLORE only after documented family uncertainty or a failed repair. Maintain a small immutable evidence ledger rather than the full lane/slot/probe machinery. This would beat v4 for small artifacts where one strong pass usually suffices and orchestration overhead dominates. It retains the same completion standard and total-cost accounting. The reported pilot motivates testing this default but does not establish its superiority for derivations or wider scientific search.

## 10. What not to import

- [design-only] Do not import autonomous objective, rubric, or operator-playbook rewriting during an evaluation cohort. Propose changes separately, authorize them, and evaluate under a new contract; improved scores under changed rules are not evidence of candidate improvement.
- [design-only] Do not import full replica-exchange machinery, bandit ensembles, large novelty models, or elaborate quality-diversity archives merely to justify the architecture's vocabulary. First show that the smaller lane/root policy fails at an observed budget. A mandatory family count is a coverage constraint, not scientific validation of the selected families.
- [design-only] Do not import a fourth recursive calibration agent swarm. Instrument calibration should be a bounded gate with independent controls and a named decision owner. Otherwise the evaluator's evaluator creates the same cost and regress problem one level higher.
- [design-only] Do not import every upstream derivation-lab stage, persona, ontology, or policy document. Pin only the claim states, assumption obligations, evidence interfaces, and transition rules required by the local contract. A reference to an unavailable external policy is not an executable local guard.
- [design-only] Do not import human approval for every routine probe rotation or route. Preserve the stated human authority, but allow explicit advance authorization of a bounded lifecycle policy; retain individual approval for changes outside it. Otherwise boundary-only updates can still freeze the bank through approval latency.
- [design-only] Do not import a judge panel everywhere or count a cross-family reviewer as automatically independent. This review has seen the design and provides development feedback. Final assessment requires separate inputs, exposure controls, and an unchanged rubric; family diversity alone does not create a holdout.
- [design-only] Do not import claims of recursive self-improvement, general superiority, or "scientific progress" from a growing archive, more agents, pairwise wins, or front movement. Preserve separate outcomes for search coverage, verified artifact improvement, instrument changes, operational reliability, and cost. No architecture change upgrades scientific status.
- [design-only] Do not import distributed infrastructure beyond the failure surface. A local append-only ledger plus dispatch/commit enforcement and resumable call identities can address the named risks; a service mesh, consensus system, or universal multi-agent bus is not justified by this brief.

[design-only] Finally, amend validation claims before running them: Stage 1 cannot establish that original rules were enforced merely by replaying headers, and absent original calibration records are not proof of invalid activation. Stage 3's single roots/depth comparison can describe one outcome but cannot establish a general winning allocation. Keep false completion as a primary outcome while also reporting failure to complete valid artifacts, unresolved required claims, coverage, and total cost; an always-halt controller otherwise looks perfect.
