## Findings

- F1. Visits rose from 4,210 in Q2 to 4,850 in Q3 (final logs, source A).
- F2. Monthly Q3 visits were 1,540 (July), 1,620 (August) and 1,690 (September) (source A).
- F3. The rise coincided with three July changes (second intake desk, longer walk-in hours, new scheduling system); no analysis attributes it to any one of them (source D).
- F4. Staffing rose from 21.0 to 23.5 FTE (source B); visits per FTE rose from 200.5 to 206.4.
